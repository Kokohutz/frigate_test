# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

| Version | Supported |
|---------|-----------|
| Latest release | :white_check_mark: Supported |
| Older releases | :x: Not supported — please upgrade |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not open a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities.**

Send an email to **security@argus-nvr.example** with the subject line:

```
SECURITY: <brief summary of the issue>
```

Include in your report:
- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce or a proof-of-concept (if safe to share)
- The version(s) of Argus NVR affected
- Any suggested mitigations you have identified

### Response SLA

| Severity | Acknowledgement | Patch |
|----------|----------------|-------|
| Critical | Within 48 hours | Within 14 days |
| High | Within 48 hours | Within 30 days |
| Medium/Low | Within 5 business days | Best effort |

We will keep you informed of our progress throughout the remediation process. If we cannot meet the patch timeline for a critical issue we will notify you with a revised estimate and explain why.

## Out of Scope

The following are **not** considered in-scope for our security bug bounty or responsible-disclosure program:

- Denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks
- Social engineering or phishing attacks against Argus NVR staff or users
- Vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies that we cannot patch ourselves (please report these upstream)
- Issues that require physical access to the server running Argus NVR
- Findings from automated scanners without a clear proof of exploitability
- Known limitations that are documented as intentional design decisions
